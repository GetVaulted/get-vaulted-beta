Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Get Vaulted (“we,” “us”) operates a collector marketplace and live commerce platform. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our mobile app and related services.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (email, username, profile information).
  • Listing, transaction, and trade activity needed to operate the marketplace.
  • Device and usage data for security, analytics, and app performance.
  • Shipping-related information when you buy, sell, or trade (addresses used for labels and delivery).

How we use information

  • Provide authentication, listings, checkout, trades, and customer support.
  • Calculate shipping rates and generate labels through integrated carriers (e.g. Shippo).
  • Process payments through our payment partners (e.g. Stripe).
  • Prevent fraud, enforce policies, and comply with law.

Sharing

We share data with service providers that help us run the platform (hosting, payments, shipping, email). We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the platform.

Retention & security

We retain information as long as needed to provide services and meet legal obligations. We use industry-standard safeguards; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may update profile information in the app and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some data must be retained for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Children

Get Vaulted is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region).

Contact

Questions: privacy@getvaulted.app
