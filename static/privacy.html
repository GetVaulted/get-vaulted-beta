Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Get Vaulted (“we,” “us”) operates a collector marketplace, live commerce platform, and mobile application. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our services.

The full, current Privacy Policy is maintained at shopgetvaulted.com/privacy. If this page differs, the website version controls.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile data (email, username, optional phone if you provide it, Apple/Google sign-in IDs).
  • Shipping and ship-from addresses, including validation through Shippo.
  • Payment and payout metadata via Stripe (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Orders, bids, trades, layaways, live purchases, chat, and support messages.
  • Listing photos, livestream participation, and public content you post.
  • Device, log, and push notification tokens (Expo) when you enable mobile alerts.

How we use information

  • Operate marketplace, live shows, trades, wallet checkout, and seller tools.
  • Process payments, tax, payouts, refunds, and disputes (Stripe).
  • Quote shipping, validate addresses, create labels, and provide tracking (Shippo and carriers).
  • Deliver live video (Amazon IVS), email (Resend), and push notifications (Expo).
  • Prevent fraud, enforce policies, and comply with law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data with service providers that help us run the platform, including Stripe, Shippo, Supabase, Netlify, Resend, AWS (IVS), Expo, and Apple/Google when you use their sign-in services. We share shipping details with counterparties when needed to fulfill orders and trades. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the platform.

Your choices

Update profile and wallet settings in the app or on the web. Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing support@shopgetvaulted.com. Some data must be retained for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Children

Get Vaulted is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
